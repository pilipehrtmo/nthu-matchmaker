Privacy notice

NTHU Matchmaker is a student class project (Team 22). This notice explains what we store and why, in line with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act.

What we collect

  • Your email, full name and student ID, to create one verified account per student.
  • Your bio, skills, faculty, weekly hours, contacts (LINE, Instagram, Discord or other) and optional professional links (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, Behance or Dribbble, Google Scholar or ORCID), to match you with teammates.
  • Posts, comments and votes you make in Discuss. They are visible to logged-in students under your full name.
  • Research documents a team uploads (proposals, papers, slides, datasets), each version with who uploaded it and when, kept in private file storage.
  • Notifications about your projects, and your choice of notification emails (off by default).
  • A sign-up log (email and time) to investigate abuse.

Who can see it

  • Other students see your full name, the projects you lead or have joined (with your role), bio, skills, faculty and the professional links you choose to add.
  • Your contacts (LINE, Instagram, Discord, other) are shown only to a team lead or applicant after an application is accepted.
  • Research positions: when you apply for a professor's research position, that professor sees your profile, skills, links and note. Only if they accept you do they also see your email and contacts, and you see theirs.
  • Research supervision: feedback threads and milestones on a project are seen only by the team, its accepted advisors and admins. An advisor's feedback becomes public only when the advisor chooses that.
  • Research documents: “Only me” drafts are seen only by the person who uploaded them; team documents by the team, its accepted advisors and admins. A document is public (to logged-in users) only if the uploader or project lead publishes one chosen version. Files are never given a public web address: every download is checked first.
  • Admins (the project team) can also see your student ID and email. We do not share data with anyone else or sell it.

How we use AI

Matching runs on the project's own computer using a local model. Your profile text is not sent to any cloud AI service.

If you use Fill in from your CV, the file is read once on the project computer to suggest profile fields, then discarded. We do not store the CV, and nothing is saved until you press Save.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-up logs are deleted after 180 days.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, feedback, replies and document versions you added to other people's projects stay so the team keeps its history, but your name is replaced by “Former member”. Your “Only me” drafts are deleted. You can delete individual messages first if you want them gone.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time, and delete your account (with your name, student ID, projects and applications) from the Profile page. Questions: [team email].

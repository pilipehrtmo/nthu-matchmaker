使用說明

NTHU Matchmaker 幫你為比賽、新創、研究和課堂專案找隊友。每個專案都列出需要的職位，你直接申請適合的職位。

加入團隊

  1. 建立帳號。按註冊，選「學生」，填入清大信箱、全名和學號。
  2. 加上你的技能。打開個人檔案，用逗號分隔輸入幾個技能，例如 figma, python, 簡報。按「儲存個人檔案」。也可以上傳履歷，系統會幫你填好。
  3. 找專案。首頁列出所有還有空位的專案。可以搜尋或篩選，每張卡片都會顯示你和最適合職位的配對度。
  4. 申請職位。打開專案，選一個職位，按「申請這個職位」，寫一兩句你為什麼適合，然後按「送出申請」。
  5. 等待回覆。隊長接受或拒絕時，上方的鈴鐺會亮起。你的申請都在動態裡。被接受後就能看到隊長的聯絡方式（LINE、Instagram 等）。

為自己的專案找隊友

  1. 刊登專案。按+ 刊登專案，選類型（比賽、新創、研究、課堂等），填標題和簡短說明。
  2. 列出職位。每需要一個人就加一個職位：名稱（例如 UI 設計師）、技能和要求。
  3. 發布。專案會出現在首頁，技能相符的同學也會在推薦中看到。
  4. 接受申請。有人申請時鈴鐺會亮起。打開你的專案，每位申請者會列在職位下方並附上留言。按「接受」或「拒絕」。被接受的成員可以看到彼此的聯絡方式。

小提醒

  • 配對度是比較你個人檔案上的技能和職位需要的技能。個人檔案沒有技能就不會顯示配對度。
  • 聯絡方式在你被接受加入團隊前都不會公開。
  • 比賽和資金在首頁和資金頁。收藏後，截止前會提醒你。
  • 討論區可以發問，或在發布專案前先找人。
  • 教授註冊時選「教授或指導教授」並完成驗證，之後就能指導專案、審閱文件和發布研究職缺。
  • 語言：在你名字下方的選單切換 English 和繁體中文。

How it works

NTHU Matchmaker helps you find teammates for competitions, startups, research and class projects. Each project lists the exact seats it needs, and you apply for a seat.

Join a team

  1. Create an account. Press Sign up, choose Student, and enter your NTHU email, full name and student ID.
  2. Add your skills. Open Your profile and type a few skills separated by commas, such as figma, python, pitching. Press Save profile. You can also upload your CV and we fill them in.
  3. Find a project. The home page lists every project with open seats. Search, or use the filters. Each card shows your match % for its best seat.
  4. Apply for a seat. Open the project, pick a seat, press Apply for this seat, write one or two sentences about why you fit, and press Send application.
  5. Wait for the answer. The bell at the top lights up when the lead accepts or declines. Your applications are listed in Activity. Once you're accepted, you see the lead's contacts (LINE, Instagram...).

Find teammates for your own project

  1. Post a project. Press + Post a project. Choose the type (competition, startup, research, class...), give it a title and a short description.
  2. List the seats. For each person you need, add a seat: its name (for example UI designer), the skills, and what is required.
  3. Publish. Your project appears on the home page, and students with matching skills see it in their recommendations.
  4. Accept applicants. When someone applies, the bell lights up. Open your project: each applicant is listed under the seat with their note. Press Accept or Reject. Accepted members see each other's contacts.

Good to know

  • Match % compares the skills on your profile with the skills a seat asks for. No skills on your profile means no match %.
  • Contacts stay private until you are accepted into a team.
  • Competitions and funding are on the home page and on Funding. Save one to get a reminder before it closes.
  • Discuss is the forum: ask questions or look for people before you post a project.
  • Professors sign up as Professor or advisor and verify their account. After that they can supervise projects, review documents and post research positions.
  • Language: switch between English and 繁體中文 in the menu under your name.
